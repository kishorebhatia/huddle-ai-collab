A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
pull up a chair.

Is every room its own workspace?

Yes. Each room has its own conversation, shared work, Brain and agent jobs. Share the room’s link to bring people into the same space.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Huddle runs in your browser. Open the room link and join your team there.

What do the agents do?

Huddle can run research and prototype jobs, show their progress, and bring outputs back for feedback. The room’s autonomy settings control when work needs approval, and you can stop agent work.

Can I try it now?

Yes. This is an early version. The “Open the room” links on this page open a shared demo room. The interactive illustration above uses sample content; it doesn’t record or run agents.
